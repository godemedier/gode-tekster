# Welcome to Gode Tekster

Hi, and thanks for giving Gode Tekster a try.

My name is Troels Kølln. I made Gode Tekster because I wanted a writing app that makes it easy for me to focus on writing something great, and not much else.

Gode Tekster is built around my own habits as a journalist, so some things will probably seem strange to you. Please write to me when that happens. Then I'll fix it (maybe).

## What you need to know to get going

Your text saves itself while you write. That's why there is no Save button.

Gode Tekster is made so you can do everything with the keyboard. For example:

- **Bold** with Ctrl+B and *italics* with Ctrl+I.
- Headings with Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2 and Ctrl+3. Ctrl+0 turns the line back into body text.
- A bulleted list like this one with Ctrl+Shift+8.
- A link with Ctrl+K. Hold down Ctrl and click to open it.

> A quote with Ctrl+Shift+Q.

If you select some text with the mouse, the same buttons pop up right above the selection.

## What the other apps don't have

{--You can dim sentences you're unsure about with Ctrl+Shift+D.--}

Dimmed sentences are left out when you print, and they don't count in the word count. But they're still there if you change your mind.

If a paragraph has to go completely, Ctrl+Alt+X moves it to the Clippings tab. You can also drag it with the mouse to the right side of the screen. And of course you can always bring it back again.

<!-- You make a comment to yourself with Ctrl+Alt+N. -->

You make footnotes with Ctrl+Alt+F.[^1]

Press F1 to see all the shortcuts in one place.

## Help from a language model

If you have Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, Gode Tekster can use your own subscription to trim your text, fact-check it and clean up interview notes. You set it up in Settings (Ctrl+,). The rest of the app works fine without it.
